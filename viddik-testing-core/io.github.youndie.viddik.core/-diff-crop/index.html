DiffCrop

object DiffCrop

The changed part of a comparison at a size it can be read at (#79): for each group of nearby DiffResult.regions, one row of three panels — the expected image, the actual one and the diff — cut to the group plus CROP_MARGIN, side by side, at 1:1 and scaled up (nearest neighbour, whole factors) when the group is small.

A full screen pair is 1080×2400 twice, and a change of one or two pixels in it is invisible once the picture is scaled down to fit a viewer, which is what an artifact viewer does and what a vision model does with a large image. The three full-size PNGs stay beside it; this is the one to open first.
